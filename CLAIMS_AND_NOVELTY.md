# Claims and Novelty Framing

This document records the category and method framing for **Verified AI Operations**. It is not legal advice and does not guarantee IP rights.

## Category claim

**Verified AI Operations** is an AI infrastructure category for systems that preserve the normal assistant experience while routing answers, tool use, agent work, datasets, and future robot actions through verification, provenance, ownership checks, completion proof, and tamper-evident audit records.

## Method claim 1 — AI output verification by refinery

A method for generating AI responses where one or more AI systems produce draft outputs, but those outputs are not returned directly. They are passed through a verification layer that extracts claims, compares them to evidence graph nodes, checks contradictions, assigns verification status, and records an audit event.

## Method claim 2 — Normal chat with verification badge

A user interface method that preserves a familiar chatbot experience while adding a minimal visible verification status: Verified, Partially Verified, Not Verified, Insufficient Evidence, Action Not Completed, or Refused for Safety.

## Method claim 3 — Hosted models as candidate engines

A method where hosted LLMs, web tools, APIs, and coding tools are treated as candidate-generation systems, while final truth status is determined by a user-controlled verification/refinery system.

## Method claim 4 — Completion-proof enforcement

A method that blocks or downgrades completion language unless an audit record proves the claimed action occurred.

## Method claim 5 — User-owned raw record architecture

A method for storing AI interaction evidence, graph nodes, audit logs, settings, and verification records in user-controlled storage so users can inspect, challenge, export, and preserve their own operational record.

## Method claim 6 — Export reconciliation standard

A method requiring AI systems to report which operational record categories are included, excluded, transformed, deleted, withheld, or unavailable.

## Method claim 7 — Synthetic-noise resistant datasets

A method for creating training/evaluation datasets that distinguish human-original, sensor-verified, business-record-verified, synthetic, synthetic-suspected, completion-verified, completion-false, and human-review-required records.

## Method claim 8 — Robotics action verification

A method preventing autonomous physical action unless task state, authority, sensor evidence, safety envelope, verification score, abort path, completion proof, and tamper-evident audit record are present.

## Publication intent

This repository documents the method so it can be cited, timestamped, and used as a public reference for Resolution Assurance and Verified AI Operations.
